My Top 5 Movies

1. The Wolf of Wall Street

Year: 2013 | Rating: 9.5/10

It is based on a true story of Jordan Belfort. It's a dark comedy film portraying how Belfort became a wealthy stock-broker.

2. The Godfather

Year: 1972 | Rating: 9.2/10

The aging patriarch of a crime dynasty transfers control to his reluctant son. I love the contrast between michael Corleone and his father's personalities.

3. The Dark Knight

Year: 2008 | Rating: 9.0/10

Batman faces the Joker, a criminal mastermind who plunges Gotham into chaos.

4. Inception

Year: 2010 | Rating: 8.8/10

A thief who steals secrets through dreams is given a chance at redemption.

5. 3 Idiots

Year: 2009 | Rating: 8.4/10

Two friends search for their long-lost college buddy, recalling their college days.
